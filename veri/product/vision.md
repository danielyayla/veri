Veri is the system of record for product intent in an agent-built
software world.

When implementation commoditizes, the scarce input is no longer code —
it is the quality of the product model humans maintain: evidence,
intent, tradeoffs, and validated outcomes. Most software tools are
strongest at the HOW; that is exactly the wrong place to compete. Veri
is strongest at the WHY, the WHAT, and DID IT WORK.

The operating principle: **humans define and revise intent; agents
execute within intent** ([[DEC-111]]). The human is the editor of
reality — discovering the real problem, interpreting messy evidence,
choosing tradeoffs, defining acceptable outcomes, and judging whether
what shipped actually solved the problem. The agent absorbs
exploration, planning, coding, migration, testing, and mechanical
verification. Veri is where the human's model of the product and its
reality lives — gated where it is authored, derived where it is
computed, checkable everywhere.

A work order here is not a ticket telling an agent what files to
modify. It is the smallest bounded product change we are currently
confident is worth making — answerable to intent on the way in and to
reality on the way out.
