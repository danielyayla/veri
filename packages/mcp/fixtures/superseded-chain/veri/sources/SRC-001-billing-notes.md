EXCERPT-START-MARKER: notes from the billing workshop. Customers want PDFs
they can forward to their accountants without editing. Line items need
quantity, unit price, and a running total; taxes are computed per line and
summed. Discounts apply before tax. Currency is fixed per invoice, never
mixed. Numbering must be sequential per year with no gaps, which rules out
generating numbers client-side. Refunds reference the original invoice
number. Layout-wise, the logo sits top left, totals bottom right, and the
footer carries payment terms. Everything after this sentence is filler to
push the body well past the excerpt cutoff so truncation is observable in
tests, and it keeps going for a while longer than anyone would reasonably
read. END-MARKER
