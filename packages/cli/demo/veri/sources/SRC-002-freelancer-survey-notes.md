Notes from a 31-response survey of freelancers on invoicing tools.

- 84% rate an offline-capable tool as "must have" — the strongest signal
  behind [[REQ-001]].
- Top feature request: locale-correct currency formatting (mentioned by
  19 of 31).
- 26 of 31 keep client records in the same tool they invoice from.
- Median invoices per month: 6. The volume argument for automation is
  weak; the accuracy argument is strong.
