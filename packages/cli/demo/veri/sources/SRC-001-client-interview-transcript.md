Transcript of four freelancer interviews on invoicing pain.

Key pulls:

- Three of four send letterhead PDFs to agencies — the template
  requirement in [[REQ-002]] came straight from here.
- All four re-key hours from a time tracker by hand at the end of the
  month, and two called it their most-hated task.
- Nobody wants an account: "the last invoicing tool I tried wanted a
  login before I could type a number."

> "I invoice from the train, from client basements, from a ferry once.
> If it needs wifi it's dead to me."
> — interviewee 2

> "The agency bounces anything that isn't on my letterhead. I paste the
> numbers into a Word template like an animal."
> — interviewee 4
